Referral Indications

Which cases to refer

Dr. Satish accepts referrals across all five of his subspecialty areas. The cases below are particularly suited to his expertise.

Orthopaedic Oncology

Bone & Soft Tissue Tumours

  • Suspected or confirmed primary bone tumour
  • Soft tissue sarcoma, diagnosis or surgical planning
  • Pathological fracture or impending fracture in an oncology patient
  • Metastatic bone disease requiring stabilisation
  • Second opinion on biopsy or surgical approach
  • Limb salvage feasibility assessment
Joint Replacement

Arthroplasty & Revision

  • End-stage knee or hip arthritis, primary replacement
  • Failed or painful joint replacement, revision assessment
  • Periprosthetic joint infection
  • Instability or dislocation after arthroplasty
  • Tumour megaprosthesis following oncological resection
Sports & Arthroscopy

Ligament & Joint Surgery

  • ACL / PCL rupture, surgical reconstruction
  • Meniscal tear, repair or reconstruction
  • Rotator cuff tear, partial or full thickness
  • Shoulder instability, recurrent dislocation
  • Cartilage defects, resurfacing options
Complex Trauma

Fractures & Non-unions

  • Intra-articular fractures, distal femur, tibial plateau, pilon
  • Periprosthetic fractures around existing implants
  • Non-union or malunion of long bones
  • Post-traumatic arthritis requiring reconstruction
  • Pathological fractures in metabolic bone disease
Upper Limb

Shoulder & Elbow

  • Shoulder arthritis, anatomic or reverse arthroplasty
  • Elbow replacement, primary or revision
  • Upper limb reconstruction post-oncological resection
  • Post-traumatic stiffness or malunion around shoulder/elbow

The Referral Process

How to refer a patient

The process is kept deliberately simple. There is no formal referral form, a call, WhatsApp message, or brief letter is sufficient to initiate a consultation.

1

Contact Dr. Satish directly

Call or WhatsApp +91 70755 23360. For urgent oncology cases, WhatsApp is usually the fastest route to a same-day response.

2

Share relevant reports

Send imaging (X-rays, MRI, CT), biopsy reports, and any prior surgical notes via WhatsApp or have the patient bring them to the appointment.

3

Patient is booked promptly

Urgent oncology and trauma cases are typically seen within 24–48 hours. Elective cases within the week. The patient will receive a confirmation of their appointment time.

4

Feedback to referring doctor

After the consultation, a clinical summary is shared with the referring doctor. For ongoing cases, updates are provided at key decision points.

Referral Documents

What to send with the referral

The more information provided upfront, the more useful the consultation will be. For oncology cases in particular, having imaging and histology before the appointment allows for a fully informed discussion with the patient on the first visit.

Where reports are not yet available, the patient can be seen and the appropriate investigations arranged from the clinic.

✦
Imaging, plain X-rays
Weight-bearing views where relevant; both sides for comparison if useful
✦
Cross-sectional imaging, MRI or CT
Essential for oncology and complex trauma cases; CD or digital link preferred
✦
Histopathology reports
Biopsy results, including receptor status where available for metastatic cases
✦
Prior surgical records
Operative notes and implant details for revision and reconstruction cases
✦
Referral letter or clinical summary
A brief note on the presenting complaint, relevant history, and what you are seeking
