Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: 28 July 2026

The information on drgsreddy.com is for general education. It is not personalised medical advice and is not a substitute for consulting a qualified doctor about your own situation.

No doctor-patient relationship through this website

Reading this site, using a tool, or sending an enquiry does not create a doctor-patient relationship. That begins only after a proper consultation and examination.

Second opinions are based on what you share

A second opinion or assessment given in response to a form or message is based on the information and images you provide. It does not replace an in-person examination, and the final plan for any patient is decided in consultation.

Decision tools are aids, not diagnoses

The decision aids help you think through options and prepare questions. They do not diagnose any condition and do not tell you whether to have surgery. Use them to inform a conversation with your doctor, not to replace it.

No guarantee of outcomes

Orthopaedic outcomes vary from person to person. Nothing on this site is a promise or guarantee of any particular result.

In an emergency

This website is not for medical emergencies. If you have severe pain, a suspected fracture, loss of sensation or movement, or any other urgent problem, go to your nearest hospital or call emergency services.

Always consult a qualified doctor

Do not delay seeking medical advice, or disregard advice you have been given, because of something you read here.
