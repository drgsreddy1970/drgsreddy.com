Patient Education · Interactive Tools

Clinical Decision Tools

Structured, interactive tools that help you think through your treatment options before your consultation. Answer a few questions, understand what matters in your situation, and walk into your appointment prepared to have a better conversation.

These tools prepare you. They do not diagnose you.

No online tool can replace clinical examination and imaging. These decision aids exist to help you organise your thoughts, understand the full range of options including non-surgical ones, and identify the questions worth asking. Every option presented, conservative or surgical, is a legitimate choice depending on your circumstances. The final decision is always made together with your surgeon, based on your specific case.

How these tools work

  1. Each tool starts with a safety check to rule out red-flag symptoms that need urgent attention rather than a decision aid.
  2. You answer structured questions about your symptoms, their impact on your daily life, and your personal priorities.
  3. The tool presents your options side by side, conservative and surgical, with honest information about each.
  4. You receive a summary of your answers to bring to your consultation, so the discussion starts from a clear picture of your situation.
