The process

How it works

From submission to specialist opinion in four steps.

1

Submit your case

Fill in the form below. Upload your imaging, reports, and a description of your concern. The more detail, the more useful the opinion.

2

Specialist review

Your case is reviewed by a fellowship-trained specialist from Dr. Satish's team, matched to your specific condition and area of concern.

3

Written opinion

You receive a written response covering diagnosis, treatment options, our recommendation, and any red flags that need urgent attention.

4

Follow-up if needed

A video consultation can be arranged if questions remain. You are always welcome to attend in person at KIMS Hospital, Secunderabad.
