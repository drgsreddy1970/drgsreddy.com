About Dr. Satish

A surgeon shaped by three continents

Dr. Satish Reddy Gandavarapu trained at some of the world's most respected orthopaedic institutions, the Rizzoli Orthopaedic Institute in Bologna, the Royal Orthopaedic Hospital in Birmingham, and Flinders Medical Centre in Adelaide, before returning to build a subspecialty practice in Hyderabad that has changed outcomes for hundreds of patients.

He founded Prime Hospitals, which later became Aster Prime Hospital, attracted private equity, and exited by selling his stake to the Aster Group in 2022. Today he brings that same ambition to his clinical practice at KIMS Hospital, Ministers Road, Secunderabad.

Areas of Expertise

Specialties

Subspecialty-trained surgical care across joint replacement, oncological reconstruction, sports surgery, trauma, and upper limb conditions.

Orthopaedic Oncology

Pioneer of bone and soft tissue tumour surgery and limb salvage procedures in AP & Telangana. Fellowship-trained at Rizzoli Institute (Bologna) and Royal Orthopaedic Hospital (Birmingham).

Joint Replacement

Total knee, total hip, and complex revision arthroplasty. Using latest implant technology and fast-track recovery protocols to return patients to full function.

Sports Injuries & Arthroscopy

Keyhole surgery for ACL/PCL reconstruction, meniscal repair, rotator cuff repair, and shoulder instability. Athlete-centred rehabilitation from day one.

Complex Trauma

Intra-articular fractures, peri-prosthetic fractures, non-unions, and mal-unions. Trained in AO trauma principles with hands-on surgical experience across three continents.

Upper Limb Surgery

Shoulder arthroplasty, elbow surgery, and upper extremity reconstruction, including post-traumatic and post-oncological cases requiring complex soft tissue management.
