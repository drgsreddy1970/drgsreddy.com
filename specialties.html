Arthroplasty

Joint Replacement

Total joint replacement is one of the most reliably successful procedures in medicine, but outcomes depend heavily on surgical precision, implant selection, and rehabilitation. Dr. Satish has performed thousands of primary and revision arthroplasty procedures, using the latest techniques including robotic-assisted surgery and fast-track recovery protocols.

His arthroplasty training at Flinders Medical Centre in Adelaide (Villis R Marshall Fellowship) exposed him to high-volume practice in total hip, total knee, and complex revision surgery under internationally recognised faculty.

Robotic-assisted joint replacement allows the surgical plan to be built around the patient's own anatomy before the first incision is made. During the procedure, the robotic system guides bone cuts and implant positioning to within a fraction of a millimetre, far beyond what is achievable by hand alone. The result is a more accurately placed implant, better preservation of surrounding soft tissue, reduced blood loss, and in most cases a faster, more comfortable recovery.

Arthroscopy & Sports Medicine

Sports Injuries

Sports injuries range from simple soft tissue strains to complex ligament reconstructions requiring precise surgical technique and structured rehabilitation. Dr. Satish treats athletes and active individuals of all ages, from recreational joggers to competitive sportspersons, with a focus on returning to full function as quickly and safely as possible.

Keyhole (arthroscopic) techniques allow most procedures to be performed with small incisions, shorter hospital stays, and faster recovery times compared to open surgery.

Fracture Surgery

Complex Trauma

Complex fractures, particularly those involving joint surfaces, those that have failed to heal, or those occurring around existing implants, require a different level of surgical planning and technical expertise than routine fracture care. Dr. Satish is an AO Trauma Accredited Faculty member, trained in the principles and practice of operative fracture management across three countries.

He manages both acute trauma and its consequences, non-unions, malunions, and post-traumatic arthritis, with an emphasis on restoring anatomy and long-term function.

Upper Limb Surgery

Upper limb orthopaedic surgery demands a particularly high degree of anatomical precision, the shoulder and elbow must balance mobility with stability, and even small errors in implant positioning or soft tissue handling can significantly affect outcome. Dr. Satish manages the full range of shoulder and elbow conditions, including complex cases arising after oncological resection or failed prior surgery.
