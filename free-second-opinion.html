When a second opinion is worth the time

A second opinion is not about doubting your doctor. It is about being sure, on a decision you only make once. It tends to be worth it when:

Major surgery has been advised

A replacement, a spine operation or a tumour surgery is a big step. Confirming the plan before you go ahead is reasonable, not rude.

You have been given conflicting advice

Two doctors, two different plans. An independent read can tell you which fits your case, and why.

You are not sure surgery is needed yet

Sometimes the honest answer is that it can wait, or be managed another way. You deserve to hear that if it is true.

What the review covers

Send your diagnosis and any scans. Dr. Satish reviews them and comes back on the questions that actually matter to you.

What you will get a clear answer on

  • Whether the diagnosis fits what your scans show
  • Whether surgery is needed now, later, or not at all
  • What the realistic options are, including non-surgical ones
  • What recovery would actually involve for you

Areas covered

  • Bone tumours and limb salvage, a particular focus
  • Fractures and complex trauma around joints
